Runs entirely in-browser. Nothing uploaded leaves this page. Each pull posterizes the source into one tonal band per ink pass, breaks up coverage with a seeded grain field, and composites the passes bottom-to-top in stack order — with each pass's own registration drift, so overlaps and near-misses come out like a real hand-pulled multi-screen edition.
